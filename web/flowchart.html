substrate · flowchart editor

Author a character's behaviour as a flowchart: start → decisions (◆) with yes/no branches → actions (□) → end. Each tick runs from start until it reaches an end. Edit the diamonds and boxes inline; the target dropdowns rewire the edges. The graph JSON below is what the sim consumes. Save a behaviour under a name to write it to behaviours/<name>.json on the server, then load it back or insert it into another flowchart in place of a selected node. A numeric threshold may be an expression over your stats, e.g. u.sense/2.
